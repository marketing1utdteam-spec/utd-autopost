Every Shopify store owner knows the app trap: the theme was $0–100, but filters are an app, upsells are an app, mega menu is an app, sticky cart is an app.

Six months later the app bill is a second rent payment — and every app is another script slowing the site down.

Ultra is built against that math. It is a $100 multipurpose theme in the official Shopify Theme Store, holding a 93% positive rating, that ships the heavy features as native sections instead of add-ons.

Filtering: advanced filtering built into the theme, not bolted on.
Merchandising: mega menu, quick view, color swatches, sticky add-to-cart.
Revenue: upsell and cross-sell blocks, plus an age verifier.
Speed: a codebase built speed-first from the ground up.

Four presets — Grip, Harbor, Sprout, Grace — restyle the theme from tech-dark to fresh-grocery without touching the underlying structure.

The economics are simple to verify.

Mega menu apps run $10+/mo.
Quick view apps run $10+/mo.
Together that is roughly $650+ a year, against a one-time $100.

Every removed app script also helps speed. Akamai's data puts the cost of 100ms of delay at up to 7% of conversions. Google's research puts mobile abandonment at 53% past 3 seconds.

The direction of the wider industry points the same way — brands consolidating app stacks for faster stores and lower fixed costs, and Shopify itself moving popular app functions into the platform.

One payment carries the work of several apps, on a codebase built to stay fast.
