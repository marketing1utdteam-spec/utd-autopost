Three more themes — and what each one is actually for.

Impression — for stores built around a look: art, interiors, objects you want to see large before you buy. Reflex, from the Impression family, is the version built to move.

Ultra — for big catalogs and tech, where findability is the whole job: filters, specs, variants, stock.

Victory — for food and drink, where appetite is created on a screen the size of a hand.

All three are in the official Shopify Theme Store. One payment, free updates, free to trial — you only pay when you publish.

Picking a theme is less about taste than about the job the store has to do. A catalog of four thousand SKUs and a shop selling twelve hand-made pieces fail in different places — one on findability, the other on presentation.
