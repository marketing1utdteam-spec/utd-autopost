Black Friday is about ten weeks out. The stores that win it prepare now, not in November.

The spike exposes whatever is weak: a slow theme buckles, a clunky mobile flow leaks orders, a long checkout kills carts. Gymshark crashed on Black Friday when its store could not handle the load — a six-figure lesson learned in public.

Five fixes worth doing this month:
1. Pass a real speed test, on a phone, on mobile data.
2. Make everything work one-handed.
3. Turn on express pay.
4. Trim checkout fields to what you actually need.
5. Stress-test the theme itself, not just the apps.

A fast, reviewed theme handles the rush by default. The rest is preparation you can still do in September.
