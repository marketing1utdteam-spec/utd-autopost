TITLE: Shopify Theme Store 100% Rating Explained
URL: https://utdweb.team

A theme's rating in the official Shopify Theme Store is a risk signal, not a vanity badge. Every listed theme is reviewed by Shopify against strict standards for speed, code quality and accessibility, then held to that bar through updates — a 100% positive rating means real merchants ran their stores on it and it held up. Skip the cheap marketplace gamble and install something vetted, maintained and supported.
