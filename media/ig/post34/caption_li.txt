A theme's rating in the official Shopify Theme Store is not a vanity badge. It is a risk signal.

Every theme there is reviewed by Shopify against strict standards for speed, code quality and accessibility before it is listed — and held to that bar through updates. A 100% positive rating means real merchants ran real stores on it and it held up.

That is the difference between renting a gamble and installing something vetted, maintained and supported.

If you are comparing themes this week, start with where they are listed, not with how they look.
