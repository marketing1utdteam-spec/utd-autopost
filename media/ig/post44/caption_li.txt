Before you hit publish, run this 8-point pre-launch audit — the same filter we use for client stores.

1. Loads under 3 seconds on a phone.
2. Works one-handed.
3. Checkout is short, with guest and express pay.
4. Trust is visible: reviews, returns, secure cues.
5. The product page answers every buying reason.
6. Every link and form works.
7. Mobile images are sharp.
8. Analytics is installed before the traffic arrives.

Most of these are properties of the theme, not add-ons you bolt on afterwards — a reviewed theme passes them by default.

Save it and run it the day before launch, not the day after.
