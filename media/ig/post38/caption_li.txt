Premium is mostly restraint.

Noom, from the Boutique family, is built for minimal, high-end brands. The design language is quiet on purpose, because quiet is what expensive brands sound like.

Homepage: a spacious layout that gives products room to breathe instead of competing for attention.

Collections: presented gallery-style, closer to an exhibition than a catalogue grid.

Product pages: one image at a time, paired with materials and story, so each piece gets its own moment before the next.

Credibility: Stanford research found that 75% of consumers judge a company's credibility based on website design. Whitespace is not empty space here — it carries perceived value.

Pricing: $160, 100% rated, one payment, free updates.

Noom is not decoration for its own sake. It is a deliberate bet that less on the page reads as more expensive to the person buying.

The theme is built for brands where the product should do the talking and the interface should simply get out of the way.
