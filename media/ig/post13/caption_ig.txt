Choosing a Shopify theme shouldn't be a gamble.

Pick like a professional, not by vibes. Start from your catalog size and niche—a 6-product brand and a 600-product catalog need different structures. Open the demo on your phone; three-quarters of all orders happen there. Check the built-in features you'd otherwise pay apps for: filters, quick view, swatches, upsells, reviews. A handful of paid apps easily runs $50–80/month. Read the rating and changelog—themes are software. Test it free with your real products before paying anything.

Stanford found 75% of users judge credibility by design, but design that doesn't match your catalog structure works against you. Official Theme Store ratings are public and unfakeable; ours hold 100% on Allure, Victory and Boutique. Every theme in the store, including all 30 of ours, can be tried free on your own store before you pay.
