There are hundreds of themes in the Shopify Theme Store, and choosing one feels like a gamble: pick wrong and you are redoing your store in three months.

It is not a gamble if you pick like a professional — by checklist, not by vibes.

Five steps, in order.
Catalog: start from your catalog size and niche, not looks — a 6-product brand and a 600-product catalog need different structures.
Mobile: open the demo on your own phone; most buyers live there.
Features: check what you would otherwise pay apps for — filters, quick view, swatches, upsells, reviews.
Maintenance: read the rating and the developer's changelog; a theme is software, it needs updates.
Trial: test it free with real products before paying anything.

Why each step matters. Stanford found 75% of users judge credibility by design, but design that doesn't match catalog structure works against the store. Statista puts mobile's share of ecommerce around three-quarters of all orders. A handful of paid apps replacing missing theme features easily runs $50–80/month — $600+ a year on top of a "cheap" theme. Official Theme Store ratings are public and unfakeable.

Gymshark started on a basic storefront and upgraded as it scaled — the lesson is not "start cheap," it is that the theme must match the stage: they re-platformed when structure stopped fitting growth. Beardbrand credits methodical storefront iteration, not redesign lotteries.

An hour with this checklist saves a quarter of rework. Step five costs nothing — every theme in the official store can be tried free on any store before payment.
