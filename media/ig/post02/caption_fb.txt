The market context makes this urgent. The Art Basel & UBS market reports documented how online art sales share jumped during 2020–2021 to roughly a quarter of the entire art market and stayed structurally higher — collectors got comfortable buying serious pieces from a screen. Hiscox's Online Art Trade Report has tracked the same shift for years: the galleries that invested in their online presentation kept those buyers.

Real-world proof came from the top of the market: galleries like David Zwirner publicly reported that their online viewing rooms — essentially well-designed product pages for art — brought in new, younger collectors who had never walked into the physical space. The lesson scales down perfectly: presentation quality is the sales argument.
