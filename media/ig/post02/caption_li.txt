Selling art online is a trust exercise: the buyer cannot stand in front of the piece, so the website has to do what a gallery does — create quiet, confidence and desire.

Collections: clean pricing and "Top seller" badges guide the browse.

Product page: size, colour and material variants, a size guide, live stock and discount badges — every buying reason in one view.

Conversion: a "You may also like" row and a sticky add-to-cart quietly grow the order, all phone-first with Quick view, because most buyers will only ever meet the gallery on a mobile screen.
