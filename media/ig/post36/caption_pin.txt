TITLE: Shopify Holiday Checkout Checklist for Faster Sales
URL: https://utdweb.team

Getting your Shopify store holiday-ready starts with checkout speed: run a real speed test, check every step one-handed since most holiday shopping is mobile, turn on express pay, trim checkout fields with guest checkout allowed, and show delivery cut-offs and stock upfront. Unexpected cost is the #1 abandonment reason at 48% (Baymard). A fast, reviewed theme handles most of this by default. Start now, not in November.
