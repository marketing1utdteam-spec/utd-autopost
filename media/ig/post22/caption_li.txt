Opening your first Shopify store is a weekend, not a someday project.

Here is the honest six-step path.

Niche: pick one niche and 10-20 products people actually search for.

Platform: open Shopify from $29/mo — hosting, payments and security handled.

Theme: install a reviewed theme. It decides look, speed and checkout.

Catalog: add products and daylight photos.

Payments: set payments and flat-rate shipping.

Launch: publish and send your audience to a real checkout.

Over 4 million stores run on Shopify — the same tooling for a beginner as for a giant.

The step people underthink is the storefront. It is the one that converts.
