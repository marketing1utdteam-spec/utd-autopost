A new store earns trust in seconds — or loses the sale.

Seven signals decide that outcome, and none of them are luck. They are built into how a store is designed.

Design: 75% of people judge a store's credibility by design alone, according to Stanford.

Speed: 53% of visitors leave if a page takes more than 3 seconds to load, per Google.

Reviews: real reviews where people decide can lift conversion by up to 1.7x, according to Bazaarvoice.

Costs: unexpected costs are the number one abandonment reason, cited by 48% of shoppers, per Baymard.

Checkout: secure-checkout cues reassure buyers at the exact moment they hesitate.

Contact: a real about and contact section signals a business behind the storefront.

Branding: consistent branding across every page keeps the trust the first five seconds bought.

A reviewed theme ships most of this by default. The design, the speed, the checkout cues are not extras added later — they are the foundation the rest of the store is judged against.

Stores that treat these seven signals as an afterthought are asking buyers to trust a stranger. Stores that build them in from the start are simply easier to say yes to.
