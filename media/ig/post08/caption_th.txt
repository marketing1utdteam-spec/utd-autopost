Your store has about three seconds to answer one question: can I trust this place with my card?

Google found 53% of mobile visitors leave a page that takes over 3 seconds to load. That's the whole game right there.

Seven-sign self-test, one minute each: load speed, clear message above the fold, zoomable photos, visible shipping costs, working search, visible reviews, no forced registration at checkout.

Fail three or more and the theme is the problem, not the traffic.
