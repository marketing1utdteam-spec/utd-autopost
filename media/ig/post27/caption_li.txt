Fashion sells on feeling, and the storefront has to create it.

Five features every apparel store needs:

Imagery: editorial photography, not product-on-white thumbnails.

Sizing: a clear size guide on the page, because wrong size is the top return driver.

Swatches: colour and size swatches that update the photo as the shopper chooses.

Styling: shop-the-look modules that build bigger orders by showing pieces together.

Checkout: a fast mobile checkout with express pay, because most fashion traffic is on a phone.

Generic themes treat a dress like a widget — a photo, a price, a buy button, nothing more. They do not carry the emotional weight fashion buying actually needs, and they leave sizing and styling as an afterthought.

The Allure family ships all five of these features natively, out of the box, without a developer.
