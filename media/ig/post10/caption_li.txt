Why the focus on visuals and speed? Research on food imagery shows pictures of food activate reward centers and measurably increase appetite — the "visual hunger" effect, Spence et al. Akamai's retail study found every 100ms of delay costs up to 7% in conversions, which is brutal for impulse-driven categories like snacks and drinks.
