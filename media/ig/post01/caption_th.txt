DM selling works until the tenth order in one week — then it breaks, because it depends entirely on you being awake and available.

Baymard puts average cart abandonment at 70%, and that's on real stores with recovery emails. In a DM, there's no cart and no recovery — the sale just dies in "seen".

Gymshark started in DMs and social. It scaled past a billion pounds only after moving to a real storefront.

Keep the conversations, move the transactions.
