"Just start with a free theme" is the most common advice new store owners get — and it is half right.

The half nobody explains is when free stops being free, and how quietly that moment arrives.

Free themes are genuinely good at the basics. The verdict changes on the year-one bill. The standard app stack that replaces missing theme features — filtering (~$19/mo), upsells (~$15/mo), quick view (~$10/mo), mega menu (~$12/mo) — runs about $56 a month. That is $672 in the first year, recurring forever.

Then it costs you twice. Every added app injects scripts into your storefront, and Akamai's retail data prices that at up to 7% of conversions per 100ms of added latency. Google's research adds the cliff: 53% of mobile visitors abandon after 3 seconds.

So the honest comparison is not $0 against $180. It is $672 a year plus a speed penalty, against one payment.
