The fear of switching themes keeps stores stuck on slow, dated templates for years. The reality is far less scary.

Your products, orders and customer data live in Shopify, not the theme &mdash; so changing themes doesn't touch them. You install the new theme, set it up privately on a preview, move your content, and publish only when it looks right. If anything's off, your old theme is one click away. Nothing goes down, nothing gets lost.
