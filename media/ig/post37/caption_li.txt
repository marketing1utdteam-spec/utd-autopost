You're already doing the hard part — making content and building an audience. The leak is what happens next.

Sending attention to a DM or a link-in-bio list wastes it. People arrive ready to buy and hit friction.

Checkout: no clear checkout process to complete the sale.

Trust: no trust signals to reassure a first-time buyer.

Speed: a slow page that stalls the decision before it is made.

Google's data is blunt — over half leave a mobile page that takes more than three seconds, and most of the audience is on a phone. That is not a traffic problem. It is a leak at the exact moment someone is ready to pay.

A proper storefront turns existing attention into orders: fast, mobile-first, one-tap checkout. The traffic already exists. What is missing is a page built to keep it.
