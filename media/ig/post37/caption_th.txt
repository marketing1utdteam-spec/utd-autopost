You're already doing the hard part — making content, building an audience. The leak is what happens after.

Sending that attention to a DM or a link-in-bio list wastes it. People arrive ready to buy and hit friction instead. Google's data is blunt — over half leave a mobile page that takes more than three seconds, and most of your audience is on a phone.

A proper storefront turns attention into orders: fast, mobile-first, one-tap checkout.
