"Shopify, WooCommerce, or something custom?" sounds like a technology decision. It is not.

It is a decision about what you want to spend the next three years thinking about: your products, or your infrastructure.

Here is what the platform actually takes off your plate.
Hosting and uptime: infrastructure that absorbs Black Friday spikes which crash self-hosted stores every November.
Payments: Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and 3D-Secure compliance built in — no gateway integrations, no PCI audits on your side.
Security: SSL, patches and DDoS protection are the platform's problem, handled by a security team larger than most companies' entire staff.
Scale: the store that takes your first order can take your millionth without re-platforming.

Gymshark is the case that settles the argument. The brand originally ran on self-managed Magento. On Black Friday 2015 the site crashed and stayed down for hours — Ben Francis has publicly estimated around £100,000 in lost sales, and the trust cost was worse.

Custom is the right answer for a handful of businesses. For the rest, it is a bill you pay in attention.
