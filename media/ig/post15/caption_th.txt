Gymshark's Black Friday 2015 crash on self-managed Magento cost them around £100,000 in lost sales. They moved to Shopify. Next Black Friday: no hiccup. They scaled past $500M on the platform.

The lesson isn't that Magento is bad. It's that infrastructure is a full-time job — and it wasn't the job Gymshark wanted.

4 million stores already settled the platform question. What actually differentiates your store is the storefront.
