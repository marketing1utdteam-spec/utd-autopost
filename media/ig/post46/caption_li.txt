A new kind of shopper is arriving, and it is not human.

AI assistants — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity — are starting to browse, compare and recommend products for people. These agents do not see your banner. They read your store's underlying structure.

Clean product data, proper markup, fast standards-based code and clear content decide whether an AI understands your catalog and puts you in front of a buyer, or skips you entirely. Messy, bloated templates are effectively invisible to them.

This is the same work that makes a store fast for humans. The difference is that a human forgives a slow page sometimes. A machine just moves on.

A modern, standards-based theme gives you that foundation by default.
