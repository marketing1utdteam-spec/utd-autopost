Most returns come from one thing: wrong size.

It sounds like a small detail until it shows up in the return numbers every single month, quietly eating into margin.

A clear size guide placed directly on the product page fixes the problem before it becomes a return. The customer decides once, with confidence, instead of guessing, ordering two sizes, and sending one back.

Problem: sizing guesswork is one of the most common reasons a buyer opens a return request.

Fix: a size guide built into the product page itself, visible before the customer ever reaches checkout.

Result: fewer returns, happier customers, more revenue kept in the business instead of refunded out of it.

Allure is UTD's Shopify theme built around this exact logic. The size guide is native to the theme, not bolted on through a third-party app that slows the page down and adds another subscription to manage.

No app to install. No extra cost. No extra load time. The fix ships with the theme itself, ready on day one.

Allure is available in the official Shopify Theme Store, reviewed and supported by Shopify.
