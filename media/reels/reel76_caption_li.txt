Show the deal, don't just say it. A clear strike-through price and a sale tag make the saving obvious at a glance — and that nudge closes the sale.

Tastefully done in Allure.

The psychology here is straightforward. Buyers do not calculate savings in their head; they need to see the number. A strike-through price creates contrast. A sale tag creates urgency. Together they remove friction from the decision.

Allure is UTD's Shopify theme built for this exact moment. The product page displays the original price crossed out, the sale price in bold, and a discount badge that answers the buyer's first question: "How much am I saving?" That visibility works across desktop and mobile, in collections and on product pages, and it compounds through the cart where the savings remain visible all the way to checkout.

The restraint matters too. Allure does not shout the deal with neon and noise. It presents the offer with clarity and taste, which builds trust alongside urgency. The buyer feels informed, not pressured.

Every percentage point of conversion is revenue. This is how themes earn their cost.
