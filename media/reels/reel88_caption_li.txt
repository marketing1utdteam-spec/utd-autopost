New customers are expensive; keeping one is gold. A simple rewards program — points to buy, perks to return — turns one order into a habit.

Gain is UTD's Shopify theme built for loyalty and retention. The storefront supports points-based rewards at every touchpoint: customers earn on purchase, earn on referral, earn on review, and redeem against future orders without friction.

The mechanics work quietly in the background. Points display on the product page so buyers understand the reward before checkout. The account dashboard shows balance, redemption history and tier status — making the program visible and tangible. Email automation sends points notifications and redemption reminders, keeping the habit alive between visits.

Conversion: the loyalty layer transforms the checkout experience from transactional to relational. A buyer who knows they are earning points is a buyer who returns.

The theme is phone-first, because most repeat customers manage their account and redeem rewards on mobile. Collections, product pages and the checkout itself are optimized for the small screen where loyalty programs live.

Gain is not a cosmetic add-on. It is a retention machine that makes the customer feel valued at every interaction.
