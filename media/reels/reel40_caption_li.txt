Sell a lot? A plain menu buries half your catalog.

A mega-menu lays it all out instead: categories, images, featured picks, one tap away.

Navigation is the first decision a store makes for a visitor, and it happens before that visitor has read a single product description. A cluttered menu turns that decision into work. A mega-menu turns it into a shortcut.

Categories: every collection visible at once, instead of nested dropdowns that hide half the catalog behind extra clicks.

Images: visual cues sit next to each category so shoppers recognise what they want instead of reading for it.

Featured picks: bestsellers and promotions surface directly inside the menu, not buried on a homepage banner three scrolls down.

Speed: one tap moves a visitor from landing page to the exact shelf they came for, with no detour through a search bar that may or may not understand the query.

This is not a feature that requires a paid app, a third-party integration, or a workaround theme edit. It ships built into Ultra, so there is no extra clutter added to the storefront and no additional page-speed cost to carry.

For stores with a large catalog, the menu is not decoration. It is the first filter a customer meets, and it either helps them find what they came for or quietly loses them at the very top of the funnel.

Ultra is built to carry that catalog size without asking the merchant to bolt on extra tools just to keep the menu usable.
