Half your buyers use search. If it comes up empty, they leave.

Good search handles typos, shows results as you type, and filters by size, color and price in a tap. Bad search hides the best products in the catalog.

Typos: a shopper types "bleu" instead of "blue" and still gets the right results, because the engine forgives small errors instead of returning nothing.

Live results: suggestions appear as the buyer types, so the search bar starts working before the query is even finished.

Filters: size, color and price narrow the list in a single tap, turning a long catalog into a short, relevant one.

Search is often the fastest converting page on a store, yet it is usually the least designed. A blank results page reads as "this store does not have it," even when the product is one click away — buyers rarely try a second spelling or a different phrase, they simply leave.

Ultra's search is built around that failure point: typo-tolerant, instant and filterable, so the buyers who already know what they want are never the ones who bounce.
