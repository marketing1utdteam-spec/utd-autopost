Shop your store with one thumb.

Stretching to reach the menu or the buy button is a small motion, but it is exactly the kind of friction that decides whether a mobile visitor completes a purchase or gives up halfway through checkout.

The way people actually hold a phone explains why this matters.

Reach: 75% of taps happen with one thumb, which means every button, filter and call-to-action either sits inside that natural arc or it does not get touched at all.

Design: navigation, product filters and the buy button are placed within that same one-thumb zone, so a visitor never has to shift grip or stretch across the screen to move forward.

Speed: fewer stretches mean fewer hesitations, and fewer hesitations mean a shorter path from landing on a product page to finishing checkout.

Most Shopify themes are still designed desktop-first and adapted afterward, which is why menus collapse awkwardly and buy buttons drift toward the top of the screen, out of thumb's reach.

Ultra was built the other way around: mobile-first from the first wireframe, then scaled up to desktop, so the one-thumb experience is not a patch bolted on later, it is the foundation.

That is the difference between a theme that merely resizes for phones and one that was actually built for how people hold them.
