A "free" theme is not free once it needs 5–6 paid apps to become usable.

The theme costs nothing. The stack around it does not.

Apps: sliders, review widgets, mega-menus — each one a separate subscription, a separate script, a separate thing that can break.

Cost: those 5–6 paid apps add up to more per month than a premium theme costs once, and the meter keeps running long after the theme fee would have stopped.

Speed: every added app is another script the storefront has to load, and load time is exactly where slow themes lose the sale before the buyer sees the product.

Maintenance: five or six apps from five or six vendors means five or six update cycles, five or six support queues, five or six places where a conflict can quietly break checkout.

Ultra was built to remove that math. Sliders, reviews, mega-menus and the rest of the toolkit most stores end up assembling piece by piece are already built into the theme.

Paid once, in the official Shopify Theme Store, reviewed and supported like any other listing there — not rented app by app, month after month, for functionality a theme should have shipped with in the first place.

The comparison is not close once the monthly totals are added side by side against a single one-time cost.
