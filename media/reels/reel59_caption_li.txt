What this looks like in practice:

Browse time: customers stay longer when a store feels intentional, which shifts the psychology from "what's on sale" to "what speaks to me".
Conversion: longer browse time converts at a higher rate, and the theme's sticky add-to-cart and Quick view keep momentum going.
