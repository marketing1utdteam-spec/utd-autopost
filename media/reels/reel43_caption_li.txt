Real store on the Ultra theme: homepage tried to say everything and visitors bounced. Rebuilt around one clear message — bounce down 19%, people stay and shop.

The shift was architectural. A homepage that tries to say everything dilutes every message: the eye doesn't know where to land, so it lands nowhere. The visitor leaves before reading anything.

One clear message changes that. When a single value proposition sits at the center of the page — supported by imagery, testimonials, and a direct path to the product — the visitor stops bouncing and starts engaging.

The numbers tell it.

Bounce rate: down 19% after the redesign.

Engagement: visitors who stayed moved into product pages and added items to cart.

Conversion: the single-message approach removed friction and noise.
