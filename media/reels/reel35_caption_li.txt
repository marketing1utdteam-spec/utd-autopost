Don't blast video or music the second someone lands.

It is jarring, it eats data, and on mobile it is an instant close.

Autoplay removes the choice. The visitor did not ask for sound or motion, and the page decides for them before the product has even shown.

Impact: unexpected media on load reads as an interruption, not a welcome.

Data: autoplaying video and audio consume bandwidth immediately, before any content has actually been requested.

Mobile: on a phone connection, an uninvited stream is often the reason the tab closes before the scroll even starts.

Let people choose to hit play.

A calm, fast first impression keeps them. The homepage should load light, load fast, and hand control to the visitor instead of taking it away from them.

Ultra gets it right. The theme opens quiet: imagery and layout carry the first impression, and any video or audio waits for a deliberate click. Nothing autoplays, nothing pushes data before it is invited, and nothing risks the mobile close.

That restraint is not a limitation. It is the difference between a storefront that respects attention and one that spends it before the visitor has decided to stay.
