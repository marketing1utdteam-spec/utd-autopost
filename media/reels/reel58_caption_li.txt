People buy the story, not just the product. Lead with a bold message, then guide them through the collection like a lookbook. That builds a brand, not just a shop.

Gain is UTD's Shopify theme built for storytelling. The storefront opens with a hero section that captures attention and sets tone — no clutter, just clarity. Collections flow like a lookbook: large editorial imagery paired with product grids that let the narrative lead.

Product page: detailed imagery, variant selectors, and a sticky add-to-cart that keeps momentum going.

Brand building: the theme uses spacing, typography and color to create a coherent visual identity across every page, so buyers remember the story, not just the transaction.

Mobile-first: Quick view and touch-friendly navigation ensure the narrative works on every screen, where most discovery happens.

Gain is not a template. It is a selling machine that reads like a magazine.

The theme is in the official Shopify Theme Store — reviewed by Shopify, updated, supported.
