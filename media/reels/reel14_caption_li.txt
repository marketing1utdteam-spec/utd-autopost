Filter app, sticky-cart app, quick-view app — $600+ a year for features a good theme ships in the box.

Most Shopify stores end up paying for the basics twice: once for the theme, then again for a stack of apps that patch in what should already be there.

Filter app: lets shoppers narrow by size, color or price — usually a recurring subscription on its own.

Sticky-cart app: keeps the cart visible while browsing, so the buying decision never gets lost in a scroll.

Quick-view app: shows product details without a full page load, cutting the friction that costs a sale.

Add those three together and the yearly bill for "extra" features runs past $600+ before the store has sold a single unit.

Ultra removes the stack. Filtering, sticky cart and quick view are built into the theme itself — one payment, no monthly subscriptions layered on top.

That is the difference between renting functionality and owning it. A store built on Ultra keeps the same features every competitor is paying app fees for, without the recurring cost eating into margin month after month.
