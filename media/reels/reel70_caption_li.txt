Half your sales could be gifts. Gift-wrap, a message, hide the price — tiny touches that turn a product into a present.

Gain is UTD's Shopify theme built around the gift economy. The theme recognizes what other storefronts miss: a gift sale is not the same as a regular sale. The buyer is solving a problem for someone else, which changes what matters.

Gift-wrap: customers can add physical wrapping at checkout, turning a box into a present and capturing margin on a service that costs almost nothing to offer.

Message: a card or note field lets the buyer add words without leaving the storefront, so the gift arrives complete and personal.

Price: the theme can hide or obscure the amount at checkout, because knowing the exact cost of a gift creates awkwardness between giver and receiver.

AOV: these three features compound. A buyer who wraps also tends to add a message. A message makes the gift feel intentional, which justifies spending more. Retailers using gift tools see measurable uplift on order value — not from pressure, but from removing friction around an impulse that was already there.

The data is clear: gift purchases spike in November and December, but they run year-round. Birthdays, weddings, apologies, celebration — the occasions are constant. Storefronts that make gifting easy capture sales that other themes leave on the table.
