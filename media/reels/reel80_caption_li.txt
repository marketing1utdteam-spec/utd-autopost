Sell online and in person? A store locator or stockist page turns browsers into foot traffic and builds trust.

Victory is UTD's Shopify theme built for retailers managing both channels at once. The store locator sits prominently in the navigation, letting customers search by location, distance or region. Each stockist page displays address, hours, phone number and a map — everything needed to visit in person.

The conversion chain works like this:

Browse: customers explore products online, building desire through the catalog.

Discover: a nearby stockist appears in the store locator, making the next step clear and local.

Trust: seeing the product available at a physical location transforms a digital browser into a committed buyer ready to walk through a door.

The omnichannel reality is simple: online stores that hide their offline presence leave money on the table. Retailers with both channels integrated see higher conversion because the customer journey does not stop at checkout — it extends into the real world, where trust is built by proximity and presence.

Victory handles this seamlessly. The theme supports multiple location pages, integrates with maps, and displays stockist inventory status so customers know before they travel.
