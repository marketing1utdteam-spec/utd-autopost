People buy a look, not one thing. Let them shop the whole outfit from one image, one tap per piece — higher average order, effortless. Made for it in Gain.

Gain is UTD's Shopify theme built around a single insight: the customer's eye does not land on individual products, it lands on complete outfits. The browsing experience reflects this. A single editorial image becomes a gallery of clickable pieces. Each tap isolates one item from the look, adds it to the cart, and returns to the outfit — no navigation required, no context lost.

The result is measurable. A customer who arrives for one dress stays to complete the look. The average order grows not through upselling or bundles, but through friction removal — the pieces are already visible, already coordinated, already desired.

Gain is phone-first by default. Collections use lookbook grids where every image is a purchase trigger. Product pages carry the full variant range — size, colour, material — alongside live stock and quick add-to-cart. The sticky checkout button waits at the bottom of every screen.

The theme is available in the official Shopify Theme Store, reviewed by Shopify, updated and supported. Fashion and lifestyle brands use it to turn browsing into buying.
