The fastest checkout is the one they don't have to type.

Every extra field is a chance to lose the sale. A name, an address, a card number — each one is a small tax on patience, and on mobile that patience is thinner than anywhere else.

Express pay removes the typing entirely.

Shop Pay: one tap, address and card already filled from a previous purchase.

Apple Pay: the same one-tap logic, tied to the device already in the buyer's hand.

The effect shows up where it matters most. Mobile is where checkout friction is felt hardest — small screens, thumbs, autocorrect — and it is exactly there that express pay can lift conversions by a third. That is not a marginal tweak; it is the difference between a cart that converts and one that quietly empties.

This is not an add-on that a store has to configure from scratch. It is built into Ultra.

The theme ships with express pay wired in from the first setup, so the checkout is fast by default rather than fast after weeks of optimization. For a store selling on mobile, that default is the whole game: fewer fields, fewer decisions, fewer reasons to abandon a cart that was one tap from becoming a sale.

Ultra is built for that reality — checkout designed around how people actually buy on their phones, not how a desktop form assumes they should.
