TITLE: Shopify Size Guide That Cuts Returns: Boutique & Gain Themes

UTD's Shopify themes Boutique and Gain put a full size guide on the product page: sizes in three systems with body measurements, plus the model's height, measurements and the exact size she's wearing. Shoppers pick the right size the first time, which means fewer returns, less spent on shipping and refunds, and a store they trust.
