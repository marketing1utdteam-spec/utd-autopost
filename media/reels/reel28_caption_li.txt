Bigger orders without more traffic? Bundle.

Buy the phone, add the case and charger at a small discount — one tap. People happily spend more when it is convenient and feels like a deal.

That logic is the entire idea behind Ultra.

Bundle: the case and charger sit right next to the phone as a single, discounted add, so the extra purchase feels like part of the original decision, not a separate ask.

Upsell: the built-in upsell surfaces the next logical item at the exact moment the buyer is already reaching for their card, instead of after checkout when the intent has cooled.

Convenience: one tap replaces browsing, comparing and a second checkout. Friction is the real reason average order value stays flat even when traffic grows.

Perceived value: a small discount on the bundle reframes the whole basket as a deal rather than three separate charges, which is what actually gets the click.

Most stores already have the traffic. What they are missing is a storefront built to turn a single-item visit into a multi-item order without asking the shopper to do any extra work.

Bundles and upsells are not an app bolted on afterward — they are built into Ultra's product and cart experience by default.
