Fashion sells on feeling — bold imagery, fit and fabric up front, sizes a tap away, a story behind the piece. Gain is built for apparel brands that want to stand out.

The theme lives in the official Shopify Theme Store, reviewed by Shopify, updated and supported.

Bold imagery: the hero section commands attention with full-width photography that stops the scroll.

Fit and fabric: product pages surface material, weight, care instructions and sizing detail upfront — the questions that stop a purchase get answered before the hesitation starts.

Sizes a tap away: variant selection is instant and visual, not buried in dropdowns.

Story: collection descriptions and product copy carry the brand voice through, so each piece connects to the customer's sense of style, not just their wardrobe.

The apparel market rewards clarity over mystery. Buyers know what they want to feel like in a piece; Gain lets the brand show it rather than explain it. Every element — the typography, the whitespace, the button placement — assumes the customer is ready to buy if the product is right.
