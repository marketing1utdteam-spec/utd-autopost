Pressure builds: every month becomes a small decision to cancel or stay. Over five years, those decisions compound into thousands in fees, regardless of whether the store actually grows.

Ultra breaks that model with a single purchase.

Payment: one price, paid once.

Updates: included forever, no renewal notices.

Support: clear guides and documentation, no surprise charges.

The store owner's math changes completely. Instead of asking "can I afford another month?", the question becomes "did this theme earn its price?" — and for stores that stick around longer than a year, it almost always does. The vendor gets alignment: success means the theme keeps working, not that customers keep paying.

This is why merchants choose to stay. Not because they are trapped in a billing cycle, but because the tool solves the problem it was built to solve, and solves it without friction.
