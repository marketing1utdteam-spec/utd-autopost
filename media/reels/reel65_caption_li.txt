One question kills sales: when will it arrive? An estimated delivery date on the product page erases that doubt. People buy when they know.

Ultra demonstrates this principle across every touchpoint.

Product page: customers see estimated delivery before adding to cart, removing the uncertainty that causes hesitation.

Checkout: delivery dates appear again at payment, reinforcing confidence in the timeline.

Post-purchase: tracking and delivery estimates continue through the customer journey, reducing support requests and repeat anxieties.

The data supports this approach. Shopify stores that display delivery estimates see measurable improvements in conversion rates — the friction point of unknown timing simply disappears when addressed upfront. Customers compare across options, but they decide based on certainty. A store that answers "when?" before the question is asked wins the comparison every time.

Ultra is a Shopify theme built for merchants who understand that selling is about removing objections, not adding features. The theme prioritizes the information buyers actually need: delivery clarity, product details, trust signals. Everything else recedes.

The design works because it respects the buyer's actual concern. Speed matters, but knowing the speed matters more.
