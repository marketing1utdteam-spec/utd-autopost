Your fonts whisper your price before anyone reads a word. Thin, elegant type feels premium; chunky defaults feel cheap. The right typeface makes a $20 product look like $50.

Allure is UTD's Shopify theme built around typography as a selling tool.

The theme pairs carefully chosen typefaces with generous spacing and hierarchy. Every font choice reinforces the perception of quality before a single product description loads. This matters because price perception forms in milliseconds — before reason catches up.

The research is clear: typography influences how buyers evaluate quality and decide what something is worth. A premium typeface paired with white space and breathing room signals a premium brand. The same product, same price, different font: one looks like a bargain, the other looks like a steal.

Allure is not decoration. It is a pricing strategy that works through visual language.

The theme is in the official Shopify Theme Store — reviewed by Shopify, updated, supported.
