Your collection page is a fork in the road.

Every shopper who lands there is making a silent decision: keep browsing, or leave. There is no neutral outcome. The layout either pulls them one tap closer to checkout or gives them a reason to bounce.

Images: big, clean product photography removes hesitation before it starts. Shoppers process visuals faster than text, and a cluttered image grid reads as a cluttered brand.

Filters: smart filtering lets a shopper narrow a hundred products down to the five that matter to them, without scrolling through everything in between.

Grid: a clear, consistent grid keeps the eye moving in one direction — down the page and toward a product, not sideways into confusion.

Signals: price and rating sitting right on the card mean a shopper can shortlist and compare without opening a single product page. Every extra click is a chance to lose them; this removes that click.

No clutter, no dead ends. Every element on the page exists to move the shopper one step closer to buying, not to decorate the page for its own sake.

That is the job the Allure theme is built to do. It treats the collection page as the decision point it actually is, rather than a waiting room between the homepage and the product.
