How to choose a Shopify theme — what to actually look for.

Not the looks alone. What makes running the store easier:

Sections: flexible layouts you can rearrange without coding.

Product layouts: smart design that guides the customer eye.

Features: swatches, sizes, and a cart that opens without leaving the page.

Mobile first: that's where most customers browse, so the theme must be built for the small screen.

Seven themes are shown on real demos: Fuse, Impression, Victory, Ultra, Boutique. Together they represent 35 ready-made designs across different styles and price points, starting from $100.

Every theme can be tried free, as long as needed — payment only happens when the store publishes. No risk, no setup fees, no time pressure.
